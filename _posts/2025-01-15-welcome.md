Welcome to my personal research website! I'm excited to have this space to share my work, thoughts, and journey in X-ray astrophysics.

## What You'll Find Here

This website serves as a hub for:

- **Research updates** – summaries of ongoing projects and new publications
- **Technical notes** – occasional posts on methods, tools, and techniques I find useful
- **Reflections** – thoughts on the scientific process, career, and the broader questions that drive us

## About My Research

I work at the intersection of X-ray astronomy, galaxy clusters, and cosmology. My current focus at CEA Paris-Saclay involves developing detection algorithms for the next generation of X-ray surveys, combining observational data with sophisticated simulations to understand the physics of the most massive structures in the universe.

## Beyond the Observatory

Science doesn't exist in a vacuum. When not at the computer analysing data or writing papers, you might find me:

- Climbing rocks (both indoors and outdoors)
- Running trails in the mountains
- Recording episodes for the *Pilgrims of the Kosmos* podcast
- Reading philosophy of science

I believe these activities aren't separate from my scientific work—they're part of the same drive to explore, understand, and push boundaries.
